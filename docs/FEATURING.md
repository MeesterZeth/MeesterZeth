# Featuring-aanmelding bij Apple (Eilandrijk)

App Store Connect → **Apps** → Eilandrijk → tabblad **Featuring** (of bovenin **…** → **Featuring Nominations**) → **+ / Create Nomination**.
Vul in het **Engels** in (Apple's redactie werkt wereldwijd).

## Velden

**Nomination type:** App Launch (of "New App")

**Nomination name:**
Eilandrijk – launch of a student-made strategy game

**Publish / launch date:** 26 november 2026

**Countries or regions:** Netherlands, Belgium (en eventueel United Kingdom, United States)

**Description (wat is er nieuw / waarom bijzonder):**
Eilandrijk is a brand-new turn-based strategy game made by a first-year teacher-training student from the Netherlands. Players start with one small castle on an island of hexagons and conquer it tile by tile, balancing gold income against army upkeep. It offers real strategic depth in short 5–15 minute matches: four unit types, terrain defense, farms and towers, a defense map, and three difficulty levels including a Master mode with no undo. It plays fully offline, is available in Dutch and English, and is free to start, with a one-time Premium purchase (no subscription, no loot boxes).

**Why it's a good fit for the App Store / the story:**
The app was designed and built by a student teacher alongside his studies, with feedback from fellow students who love maths and strategy. It is a fair, family-friendly strategy game with hand-drawn art, original music and sound, an interactive tutorial, and accessibility in mind (large tap targets, adjustable turn timer, sound and music toggles). It shows how a newcomer can create a polished, original game for iPhone.

**Apple technologies / features used:**
StoreKit in-app purchases, App Tracking Transparency, haptics, native iOS review prompt, full offline play, Dutch and English localization.
