## English

**Name** (30): Eilandrijk: Island Strategy

**Subtitle** (30): Conquer the isle tile by tile

**Promotional text** (170):
Build your empire, manage your gold and outsmart clever rivals. 30 levels, three difficulties and a Master mode for true strategists. Plays offline.

**Description** (4000):
Eilandrijk is a turn-based strategy game for players who love to think ahead.

You start with a small castle on an island of hexagons. Every tile earns gold, but every soldier costs wages. Expand and you grow richer. Expand too fast and your army is stretched thin. Every turn is a real decision.

STRATEGY WITH DEPTH
• Four units, from the swift scout to the unstoppable catapult
• Forests, towers and castles defend, and only greater strength wins
• Farms, villages and wages: manage your economy or your army deserts
• Defense map: see at a glance where you are vulnerable

A CHALLENGE FOR TRUE STRATEGISTS
• 30 levels with growing islands and up to three rivals
• Three difficulties: Normal, Hard and Master
• On Master there are no take-backs, and every win earns a crown
• 8 achievements, such as "Win without losing a single tile"
• Stars for fast victories

MADE FOR YOUR PHONE
• Short matches of 5 to 15 minutes
• Turn timer (adjustable or off)
• Auto-save: pick up right where you left off
• Fully playable offline
• English and Dutch

Levels 1 to 10 are free. Premium (one-time purchase) unlocks all 30 levels without ads. No subscription, no loot boxes.
