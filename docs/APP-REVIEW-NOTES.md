1. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Eilandrijk is an offline, turn-based strategy game on a hexagon island. The player starts with one castle, expands tile by tile, manages gold and unit upkeep, and wins by capturing every enemy castle. It is aimed at teens and adults (9+) who enjoy strategy and puzzle games and want short, thoughtful matches of 5-15 minutes without a subscription or account. Its value: deep strategy (economy vs. army, terrain defense, three difficulty levels) in a format that fits a phone.

2. HOW TO USE THE APP / MAIN FEATURES
- No account, login or sample files are needed.
- Tap "Spelen" (Play). On first launch an interactive tutorial starts automatically. It can also be opened via "Tutorial" in the main menu.
- Language can be switched between Dutch and English with the "NL · EN" button in the top right of the main menu, or in Settings.
- Levels 1-10 are free. Levels 11-30 require the one-time purchase "Premium".
- In-app purchases are in the "Winkel" (Shop) button on the main menu:
  * Premium (nl.meesterzeth.eilandrijk.premium, non-consumable): unlocks levels 11-30, removes ads, +15 starting gold per level.
  * Kleurenpakket / Color Pack (nl.meesterzeth.eilandrijk.kleuren, non-consumable): 5 cosmetic empire colors, selectable in Settings.
  * "Aankopen herstellen" (Restore Purchases) is in the Shop.
- Optional rewarded ad: before a level, the player may choose to watch an ad for +15 starting gold. Free players also see an interstitial ad after every third finished level. Premium players see no ads.
- Settings (main menu > "Instellingen"): language, difficulty, turn timer, sound, music, color, ad privacy options.

3. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Apple StoreKit via RevenueCat (purchase validation and restore). No personal data is collected by the developer.
- Google AdMob (ads), with Google User Messaging Platform (GDPR consent) and Apple App Tracking Transparency.
- No servers of our own, no login, no AI services, no user-generated content, no chat.
- All game progress is stored locally on the device.

4. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app functions the same in all regions. The only difference is legally required: users in the EEA/UK see Google's consent form for ads before ads are shown. Prices are shown in local currency by the App Store. The app is not offered in China mainland.
