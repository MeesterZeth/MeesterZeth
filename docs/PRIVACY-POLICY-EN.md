# Eilandrijk Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 22 November 2026*

Eilandrijk is made by MeesterZeth. This policy explains what data the app uses.

**What we store.** Your progress (stars, achievements, settings, saved game and purchases) is stored only on your own device. We do not receive this data.

**Purchases.** Purchases are handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play. To verify and restore purchases we use RevenueCat, which uses an anonymous user ID. We never see your name or payment details.

**Advertising.** The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may process an advertising identifier and app usage data. On iOS we ask for your permission first. If you decline, you will see ads that are not personalised. Premium players see no ads.

**Children.** The app is not specifically directed at children under 13.

**Contact.** Questions? Email zethvanhalteren@icloud.com.
