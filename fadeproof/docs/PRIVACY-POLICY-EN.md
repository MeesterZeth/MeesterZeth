# Fadeproof Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 22 November 2026*

Fadeproof is made by MeesterZeth. This policy explains what data the app uses.

**What we store.** Your courses, topics, progress, settings and coins are stored only on your own device.
No account is needed. We do not receive this data and do not send it anywhere.

**Advertising.** Fadeproof only shows an ad when you choose to watch one (a video for coins or a streak saver).
These ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may process an advertising identifier, coarse location (based on
your IP address) and app usage data. In Europe we first ask for your consent through Google's consent form.
On iOS the app then asks whether it may track you. If you decline, you will see ads that are not personalised.
You can change your choice in Profile → Ad privacy settings. More information:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

**Children.** The app is not specifically directed at children under 13.

**Deleting your data.** Delete the app from your device. This removes all your data.
